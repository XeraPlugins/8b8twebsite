Frequently Asked Questions

New to the server? Here's what you need to know.

What is 8b8t?

8b8t is a player-driven survival multiplayer server. Unlike many Minecraft servers, gameplay is largely unrestricted, with minimal intervention from staff. Players are free to explore, build, compete, and shape the world through their own actions.

How do I dupe?

We feature the legendary Donkey Dupe. You can watch the official tutorial here. Note that voting is required for duping mechanics.

How do I report bugs?

Submit bug reports or suggestions to contact@8b8t.me. We usually respond within 48-72 hours.

Is this a 2b2t alternative?

We are our own community. Unlike current 2b2t or Constantiam, we strictly adhere to survival mulitplayer principles without enforcing gameplay restrictions.

Is 8b8t Pay-to-Win (P2W)?

While we offer perks to supporters, the core gameplay is balanced. You can easily thrive and compete without spending a single penny.
