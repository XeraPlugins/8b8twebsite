Our philosophy is simple: true freedom for players.

8b8t operates with little to no admin intervention, allowing the world to develop naturally through the actions of its players. There are no artificial restrictions shaping gameplay — what happens here is entirely driven by the community.

Unlike many so-called "anarchy" servers that introduced rules to comply with Mojang's EULA enforcement, 8b8t has remained consistent. The core codebase and principles that defined the server in 2016 are still intact today. We don't redefine freedom — we preserve it.

Where It Started

A decade of anarchy, three names, one identity.

2016

Born as 0b0t

Created by Leee as a non-vanilla anarchy alternative to 2b2t and its endless queue times.

2019

Rebranded to 6b6t

After losing the original domain, the server took a new name. Its map ran from March 3, 2019 until April 2020.

2020

A New World

A hardware failure took the old map offline. The current world was created on August 18, 2020 — and hasn't been reset since.

2021

8b8t Today

Reborn as 8b8t, the server carries the same world forward — six years of player history and counting.

Under the Hood

8b8t runs on a highly customized Folia fork for multi-threaded performance traditional servers can't match. Our core plugin, 8b8tCore, is the most advanced anti-illegal system ever made — protecting the server at the bitstream level.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is hacking allowed on 8b8t.me?
Yes. 8b8t is an anarchy server with no in-game rules.
Why is 8b8t a cracked server?
8b8t is a cracked server because of Mojang's EULA. We believe in player freedom, privacy and choice.
Why do illegal events exist?
Illegal events are a way to make money from the server. We do not profit off our server and our anti-illegal system allows us to revert items after the illegal event.
Why is there /homes, /tpa, etc.?
Being the very first non-vanilla anarchy server, we streamlined most of the tedious tasks. The majority of our playerbase prefers such commands as it makes it easier to make massive builds with less players.
What is the seed of 8b8t?
The seed is hashed with the Blake3 Digest algorithm — a one-way function to protect against world terrain exploitation such as block rotations, bedrock, and terrain. We keep the salt private.
Can I join 8b8t on Bedrock?
Yes, we have Bedrock Edition support using the IP Address br.8b8t.me with the Port 19132.
